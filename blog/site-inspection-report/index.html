How to Create a Site Inspection Report with GPS Photos and PDF

Record a site visit, capture time- and location-linked photographs and prepare a shareable report.

To create a useful site inspection report, combine the visit date, recorded GPS route, relevant photographs and a clear summary of the activity. Track Reporter brings route recording, photo evidence and PDF generation into one Android workflow for inspectors, contractors and maintenance teams.

What should an inspection report contain?

The report should identify the location and time of the visit, the areas covered, relevant observations and supporting evidence. Use photographs to show asset conditions or the results of a repair. Keep any required inspection checklist or compliance documentation alongside the recorded evidence.

  • Visit date, duration and location context.
  • A map showing the recorded route.
  • Photographs linked to GPS positions and times.
  • A readable report that can be shared or retained.

Create your report in three steps

1. Record the visit

Start a recording in Track Reporter when the visit begins. The app captures GPS positions and timestamps. Basic route recording works without mobile data, although position accuracy depends on the device and available satellite signal.

2. Capture photographs on site

Photograph relevant equipment, defects and completed tasks. Add useful context where available. The photo gallery connects images with the visit rather than leaving evidence scattered across messaging apps.

3. Review and export the report

Finish the recording, inspect the map and review the session details. Generate a PDF for the customer, supervisor or project file. For assigned jobs, see how to link routes to Work Orders.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a report directly on Android?

Yes. Track Reporter supports GPS recording, photographs and PDF reports.

Does a route prove the quality of an inspection?

No. It supports the visit record; inspection findings and required checks must still be reviewed.
