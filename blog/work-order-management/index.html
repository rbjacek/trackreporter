Work Order Management with GPS Route Evidence

Assign jobs, follow progress and review GPS routes and photos in the context of completed work.

Track Reporter helps small field service teams manage Work Orders and document the visits associated with them. Instead of checking a task list and a separate GPS history, managers can review the assignment, its status and recorded field activity together. The Android app supports work in the field, while the web dashboard helps managers coordinate the team.

What is a field service Work Order?

A Work Order identifies the job, the customer or site, the person responsible and the required work. Use one for a maintenance call, property check, patrol or inspection. A clearly defined assignment gives the technician a reference before departure and the supervisor a record to review afterward.

How to manage Work Orders in Track Reporter

  1. Create: enter the job number, client, site, description and due date.
  2. Assign: choose a fleet member by email. The worker finds the assignment in My Work Orders.
  3. Start: update the status and record the field visit on Android. Take relevant photos as the work progresses.
  4. Document: attach a recorded GPS route to the Work Order so the evidence belongs to the correct assignment.
  5. Complete: review the job and its attached evidence; use the completion report to communicate the result.

Follow progress and exceptions

Track Reporter organizes jobs by Assigned, In Progress, Completed, Rejected and Cancelled. Administrators can reopen jobs that need additional work. A completion status helps prioritize review; it should not be treated as proof that every required task was performed.

Why attach a GPS route?

A route records device movement and timing. Location-tagged photos add visual context. Together they help resolve questions about a site visit and support a report for a customer or supervisor. GPS alone cannot establish workmanship or certify that an inspection meets a regulatory standard.

Android and web access

Use Android to record routes and capture evidence. Authorized fleet managers can manage and review work through the browser. Basic route recording works offline; online fleet actions and synchronization require connectivity. Fleet access requires the appropriate membership and subscription entitlement.

Frequently asked questions

Can I attach a route that I already recorded?

Yes. Select a saved route and associate it with the relevant Work Order.

Is vehicle tracking hardware required?

No. Track Reporter records field activity with an Android device.
