How to Prove Field Work Was Completed with GPS Photos & Reports

Build a useful record of field work with GPS route history, location-tagged photos and a shareable report.

Proof of work in field services combines a record of where a worker went, when the activity occurred and what was documented. Track Reporter helps technicians and supervisors keep GPS routes, time-linked photos and PDF reports together, so they can review a visit without reconstructing it from separate messages and screenshots.

What counts as proof of field work?

A useful record connects location, time and visual evidence to the specific job. For example, a maintenance technician visiting three properties can record the journey and photograph each serviced asset. The resulting evidence helps a supervisor identify the visits and investigate questions from a customer.

Why GPS alone is not enough

A GPS track shows the recorded movement of a device. It cannot independently establish whether the work was completed correctly. A photograph adds context, but should be tied to the relevant location and time. Review both alongside the work requirements and any field notes.

Document field work in five steps

  1. Record the route: start GPS recording before the field activity.
  2. Capture evidence: photograph relevant equipment, conditions and work results during the visit.
  3. Keep recording: basic GPS recording works offline at locations with limited connectivity.
  4. Review the session: inspect the route map, timing and associated photos; check for gaps or missing evidence.
  5. Generate a report: export a PDF to share with the customer or supervisor.

Who benefits from GPS proof of work?

Field technicians can document service calls; inspectors can retain a visual record of visited areas; security teams can review patrols; delivery crews can document travel and related photos. For recurring assignments, a manager can connect a recorded route to a Work Order and review shared fleet history.

One workflow on Android and the web

Track Reporter supports GPS recording, route playback, location-tagged photos and PDF reporting on Android. Authorized fleet users can review shared records and live locations when members actively share them. Online fleet features require internet access.

Frequently asked questions

Does a GPS report guarantee work was completed?

No. It provides supporting evidence, not independent certification or proof of work quality.

Can I use Track Reporter on an iPhone?

The app is available for Android on Google Play; fleet review is also available in a web browser.
